DJ FLOW

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

DJ FLOW ("DJ FLOW", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DJ FLOW iOS application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

DJ FLOW stores certain information locally on your device to provide app functionality and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • Favorite tracks
  • Watchlist tracks
  • Local price history
  • DJ DNA preferences and interaction feedback
  • GOOD and SKIP feedback
  • Music display preferences
  • Onboarding status
  • Cached music metadata and search information

This information is stored locally by DJ FLOW and is not uploaded to a DJ FLOW account or DJ FLOW-operated server.

DJ FLOW currently does not require users to create an account.

2. Music Search and Metadata Services

DJ FLOW uses third-party and Apple services to search for music, retrieve music metadata, identify tracks, and provide related functionality.

Depending on the feature you use, search terms and music-related information such as artist names, track titles, identifiers, BPM, key, or similar metadata may be transmitted to services including:

  • Apple iTunes Search API
  • Apple ShazamKit
  • YouTube Data API
  • GetSongBPM
  • FreqBlog

These requests are made to provide the feature requested by the user. The handling of information by these services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

DJ FLOW does not use these services to build advertising profiles about its users.

3. Microphone Access and ShazamKit

DJ FLOW may request access to your device's microphone when you use music identification functionality.

Microphone access is used to identify music that is currently playing through Apple's ShazamKit functionality.

DJ FLOW does not operate its own server for storing microphone recordings and does not use microphone access for advertising or tracking purposes.

You can revoke microphone access at any time through iOS Settings.

4. DJ FLOW PRO and Purchases

DJ FLOW offers optional DJ FLOW PRO features through Apple's in-app purchase and subscription system.

Purchases and subscription entitlements are processed using Apple StoreKit.

DJ FLOW does not directly receive or store your full payment card information. Payment processing and Apple Account purchase information are handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

DJ FLOW may use StoreKit-provided transaction and entitlement information on your device to determine whether DJ FLOW PRO features should be available.

5. Favorites, Watchlist, DJ DNA and Price History

Favorites, Watchlist information, DJ DNA preferences, interaction feedback, and DJ FLOW's local Price History are stored locally on your device.

Price History represents observations recorded by DJ FLOW after a track has been added to your Watchlist. It should not be interpreted as a complete historical record of prices before the track was added.

DJ FLOW does not currently synchronize this local information to a DJ FLOW cloud account.

6. External Stores and Services

DJ FLOW may provide links that open third-party music stores, websites, applications, or services.

DJ FLOW is a music discovery, recommendation, and comparison tool. DJ FLOW does not sell, license, process payment for, or deliver downloadable music through these external links.

Once you leave DJ FLOW, your interaction with a third-party service is governed by that service's own privacy policy and terms.

7. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

DJ FLOW does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or third-party analytics services for behavioral advertising.

DJ FLOW does not sell personal information.

DJ FLOW does not use cross-app or cross-website tracking for advertising purposes.

If these practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by DJ FLOW remains on your device until it is removed through applicable app functionality, the app's local data is cleared, or the application is deleted, subject to iOS behavior.

Because DJ FLOW currently does not provide a DJ FLOW user account or operate a cloud account database containing your Favorites, Watchlist, DJ DNA, or Price History, there is currently no corresponding DJ FLOW cloud account to delete.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information under our control, you may contact us using the email address below.

9. Third-Party Privacy

Third-party services used or linked by DJ FLOW may independently process information according to their own privacy policies.

DJ FLOW does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites, stores, or services. We encourage users to review their applicable privacy policies before using those services.

10. Children's Privacy

DJ FLOW is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information from a child has been provided to us in a manner inconsistent with this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of DJ FLOW to protect information under our control. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DJ FLOW's features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date displayed at the top of this Privacy Policy.
